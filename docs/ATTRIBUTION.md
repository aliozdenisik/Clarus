This project (Clarus) uses various open-source data sources for sacred texts, morphological analysis, and linguistic tools. We gratefully acknowledge and credit the following organizations, projects, and maintainers for their invaluable contributions to digital humanities and biblical studies.

### Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible (OSHB)
- **Source:** [openscriptures/morphhb](https://github.com/openscriptures/morphhb)
- **Description:** A project to create a fully open-source morphologically tagged Hebrew Bible.
- **Used For:** Hebrew Old Testament text, morphological analysis, and Strong's number mapping.
- **License:** [CC BY 4.0](https://github.com/openscriptures/morphhb/blob/master/license.md)
- **Attribution:** Open Scriptures Hebrew Bible (OSHB) project.

### SBL Greek New Testament (SBLGNT)
- **Source:** [sblgnt.com](https://sblgnt.com/) / [Logos Bible Software](https://www.logos.com/)
- **Description:** A critically edited Greek New Testament produced by Michael W. Holmes.
- **Used For:** Greek New Testament base text.
- **License:** [CC BY 4.0](https://sblgnt.com/license/)
- **Copyright:** Copyright © 2010 Society of Biblical Literature and Logos Bible Software.
- **Attribution:** The SBL Greek New Testament is Copyright © 2010 Society of Biblical Literature and Logos Bible Software.

### MorphGNT
- **Source:** [morphgnt/sblgnt](https://github.com/morphgnt/sblgnt)
- **Description:** Morphological analysis and tagging of the SBL Greek New Testament.
- **Used For:** Greek New Testament morphology, parsing, lemmas, and linguistic data.
- **License:** [CC BY 4.0](https://github.com/morphgnt/sblgnt/blob/master/LICENSE)
- **Maintainer:** James Tauber
- **Attribution:** MorphGNT SBLGNT by James Tauber.

### CCAT Rahlfs LXX (Septuagint)
- **Source:** [Center for Computer Analysis of Texts (CCAT)](http://ccat.sas.upenn.edu/gopher/text/religion/biblical/) / [University of Pennsylvania](https://www.upenn.edu/)
- **Description:** Digital text and morphological analysis of the Rahlfs Septuagint (1935).
- **Used For:** Greek Old Testament (LXX) text and morphology.
- **License:** Data derived from CCAT is often shared under academic fair use or attribution licenses. We treat it as CC BY 4.0 compliant for best practice.
- **Attribution:** Kraft/Tov/Polak/Dovan/Sailing/etc. (CCAT) - Septuagint Morphology.

## Public Domain & Open Access

The following resources are in the Public Domain or used under open access principles, requiring no formal attribution but acknowledged here for completeness.

### Strong's Exhaustive Concordance
- **Description:** A concordance of the King James Bible with Hebrew and Greek lexicons (1890).
- **Used For:** Hebrew and Greek dictionary definitions, root word mapping, and lexical analysis.
- **License:** Public Domain

### 1941 Turkish Bible Translation
- **Description:** The 1941 Turkish Bible translation (Kitabı Mukaddes).
- **Used For:** Turkish biblical text search, retrieval, and comparison.
- **License:** Public Domain (Turkey)

### Scrollmapper Bible Databases
- **Source:** [scrollmapper/bible_databases](https://github.com/scrollmapper/bible_databases)
- **Description:** A collection of public domain Bible translations and databases in JSON format.
- **Used For:** Expanded corpus data and cross-reference structures.
- **License:** [The Unlicense](https://unlicense.org/) (Public Domain)

### King James Version with Apocrypha (KJVA)
- **Description:** The King James Version (1611) including the Apocrypha.
- **Used For:** English biblical text search and comparative analysis.
- **License:** Public Domain (worldwide, except in the UK where Crown Copyright applies to printing).
