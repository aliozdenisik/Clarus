Integrated Insight

The Quran emphasizes God's (Allah's) absolute sovereignty in creation. It mentions the creation of heavens and earth in six "ayyam" (days or periods) and specifically highlights that God was not touched by weariness. The focus is often on the purpose of creation as a sign for believers.

Surah Al-A'raf 7:54 open_in_new

"Indeed, your Lord is Allah, who created the heavens and earth in six days and then established Himself above the Throne."

Key Themes: Sovereignty 6 Days
Surah Fussilat 41:10 open_in_new

"And He placed on the earth firmly set mountains over its surface, and He blessed it and determined therein its sustenance..."

Key Themes: Earth Sustenance

Integrated Insight

The Bible presents a structured narrative of creation in Genesis, detailing specific acts on each of the seven days. The New Testament (John 1) reframes creation through the lens of the "Word" (Logos), emphasizing the agency of Christ in the beginning.

Genesis 1:1 open_in_new

"In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth."

Key Themes: Origin

"Through him all things were made; without him nothing was made that has been made."

Key Themes: The Word Agency

Key Themes: Humanity

Integrated Insight

The Torah narrative in Bereshit (Genesis) serves as the foundation for the Sabbath. The account emphasizes the ordering of "Tohu va-Vohu" (chaos and void) and culminates in the sanctification of the seventh day as a cessation of work.

Bereshit 1:1 open_in_new

"In the beginning of God's creation of the heavens and the earth."

Key Themes: Beginning
Bereshit 2:2 open_in_new

"And on the seventh day God finished the work that He had been doing."

Key Themes: Shabbat Completion
